## Financial authority and security proof

- Account/tenant/assigned-branch checks occur before the Finance shell; foreign branch/tenant records
  deny or 404 without enumeration. The backend remains authoritative for every request.
- Finance is checker-first and cannot create merchant-client invoices. Front Office recording and
  Finance validation stay separate; the Finance back-office recording exception remains its own key.
- Validation is atomic across the group. Original receipts follow successful validation. Reissue is
  independently permissioned. No browser-computed recognized amount or receipt state exists.
- Duplicate references remain masked; override requires the existing permission, explicit reason,
  idempotency, mandatory MFA and fresh step-up. The source reference is never overwritten.
- Dispute review is distinct from external-refund request/approval/finalization. Cash-up, refund and
  payout workflows retain maker/checker. “Marked paid” records an external reference; Servana moves
  no payout money and calls no provider.
- Locked periods refuse mutations. Reopen remains exceptional, reasoned and step-up protected.
  Compensation liabilities are currency-separated; Finance cannot change HR compensation terms.
- Export request/revoke and download are independent capabilities. Files remain scoped, masked,
  reasoned, expiring, download-counted and private. `REM-EXP-001` retention convergence stays open
  under Phase 21N; UI-12 does not schedule it.
- Gate W remains closed. No Wallet STK, callback, reconciliation, settlement, Refer & Earn,
  notification persistence, scheduled-report worker or fake provider/report/notification state was
  introduced.
