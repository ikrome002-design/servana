## 13. FAQ Section

### **Do I create my own Merchant Personnel account?**

Your access is usually activated under the merchant business account by the Merchant Administrator or authorized manager. Once active, you can log in securely using your email.

### **How do I log in?**

You log in using a Magic Link sent to your email. Your email must be active under the correct merchant account.

### **Can I see all clients in the business?**

No. Servana is designed to protect client records. Merchant Personnel can only access allowed client data, especially for clients they personally served and where system permissions allow it.

### **Can I track my commissions?**

Yes. Servana is designed to help you view commission earned and commission pending based on recorded service activity.

### **Can clients request me specifically?**

Yes. Servana supports preferred-personnel requests, allowing clients to choose a specific service provider where the merchant enables that workflow.

### **Can I download client contacts?**

Only where allowed. Contact downloads are controlled by permissions, consent rules, payment rules, and audit logs.

### **Is Servana only for salons?**

No. Servana is built for service-based SMEs, including barbershops, salons, spas, massage parlours, grooming studios, beauty parlours, and similar businesses.
