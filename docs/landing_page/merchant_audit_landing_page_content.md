## 13. FAQ Section

### What can a Merchant Audit user do?

You can view audit logs, role changes, branch changes, invoice history, payment validation logs, receipt logs, queue reassignment logs, preferred personnel fee logs, contact export logs, export audit reports, and flag suspicious activity.

### Can Merchant Audit edit records?

No. Merchant Audit access is read-only. You cannot edit clients, services, invoices, payments, receipts, users, queues, or commissions.

### How do I access my Merchant Audit account?

Your Merchant Administrator must add or approve your audit access. After that, you log in securely through a Magic Link sent to your email.

### Can I export audit reports?

Yes. Merchant Audit users can export audit reports where permissions allow.

### Does Servana process payments?

No. Servana records offline payment details and validation activity, but client-to-merchant payments happen outside the platform.

### Who is Servana built for?

Servana is built for African service-based SMEs such as salons, barbershops, spas, grooming studios, beauty parlours, massage parlours, and similar appointment or walk-in businesses.
