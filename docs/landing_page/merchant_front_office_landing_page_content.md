## 13. FAQ Section

### What happens when I click GET STARTED?

You will be directed to the login and create account flow. Merchant Administrators can create a business account, while approved merchant users can log in once their email is active under the merchant account.

### Can Front Office users create clients?

Yes. Front Office users can register new clients and retrieve existing client records.

### Can Front Office users handle walk-ins and appointments?

Yes. Servana is designed for both walk-in and appointment-based service businesses.

### Can Front Office users record payments?

Yes, where the merchant allows it. Some payments may still require Finance validation before receipts are issued.

### Does Servana process payments directly?

No. Payments are handled offline or off-platform. Servana records payment details, references, status, and validation information.

### Can clients choose preferred personnel?

Yes, where enabled. A client can choose next available personnel or wait for a preferred service provider at an extra cost where configured.

### Is Servana only for salons?

No. Servana supports salons, barbershops, spas, grooming studios, massage parlours, beauty parlours, and similar service-based SMEs.
