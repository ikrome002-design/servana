## 13. FAQ Section

### What does the Merchant Human Resource account do?

It helps you manage staff users, employment status, role assignment, branch assignment, personnel profiles, staff availability, account activation status, and approval requests.

### Can HR create and manage staff users?

Yes. HR can add, edit, deactivate, and maintain staff records based on the permissions configured for the merchant business.

### Can staff log in immediately after being added?

No. Merchant users only log in through Magic Link after their email and account access are active under the merchant’s account rules.

### Can HR assign staff to branches?

Yes. HR can help ensure staff users are connected to the correct branch, which supports cleaner access control and smoother operations.

### Is Servana only for salons?

No. Servana is built for service-based SMEs such as salons, barbershops, spas, massage parlours, grooming studios, beauty parlours, and similar appointment or walk-in businesses.

### Does Servana replace the Merchant Administrator?

No. HR supports staff and access management, while the Merchant Administrator remains responsible for wider merchant-level control, approval, configuration, and business oversight.
