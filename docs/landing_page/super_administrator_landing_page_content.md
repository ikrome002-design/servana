# 12. FAQ Section

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is Servana only a booking system?

No. Servana is built for service operations. It supports the work behind bookings, walk-ins, clients, staff, invoices, offline payments, commissions, platform fees, and audit records.

### Who is the platform dashboard for?

The platform dashboard is for you as the platform owner. It helps you manage merchants, settings, fees, reports, permissions, audit logs, and platform-level controls.

### Do clients pay through Servana?

Client-to-merchant payments are handled offline. Servana helps record payment method, amount, reference, status, and validation details.

### Can I control platform fees?

Yes. You can configure platform service fees, billing cycles, contact-download fees, and preferred-personnel waiting fees.

### Can merchants manage their own teams?

Yes. Merchants can manage their branches, staff, services, clients, queues, invoices, payments, receipts, and commissions based on their assigned permissions.

### Can clients choose a preferred staff member?

Yes. Clients can choose the next available personnel or wait for a specific personnel at an extra fee controlled by you.
