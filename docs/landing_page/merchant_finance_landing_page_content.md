## 13. FAQ Section

### **Frequently Asked Questions**

#### **Does Servana process payments inside the platform?**

No. Servana records and validates offline payments. Your clients can pay through methods such as cash, M-Pesa, bank transfer, card terminal, voucher, split payment, or another merchant-defined offline method. 

#### **Can Front Office record payment details?**

Yes, where permitted. Front Office can record payment details, but Finance is responsible for validating payment truth, especially for high-risk or merchant-configured payment states. 

#### **Can receipts be generated before payment is validated?**

Receipts should not be generated before payment is marked as received or validated, depending on the merchant’s configuration. 

#### **Can Merchant Finance see commissions?**

Yes. Merchant Finance can view commission liabilities so the business has clearer visibility over what may be owed to personnel.

#### **Is the Finance account separate from other merchant users?**

Yes. Servana uses role-based access so Finance users can focus on payment validation, records, receipts, reports, balances, commissions, and financial activity without needing full control over every merchant operation.

#### **How does a Finance user get access?**

The Merchant Administrator creates or activates the Merchant Finance user under the merchant account. The user then logs in through Magic Link after their email and role are active.
