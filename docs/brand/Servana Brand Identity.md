# SERVANA — FULL BRAND IDENTITY SYSTEM

## 1. Brand Foundation

### Primary Brand Name

# **Servana**

### Brand Name Rationale

**Servana** works because it naturally blends three strong ideas:

**Serve** — the platform exists to help service-based SMEs manage their daily operations better.

**Savannah** — it carries an African identity without sounding forced, tribal, or overly literal.

**Nirvana / ease** — it subtly suggests relief, flow, order, and calmness in business operations.

The name feels warm, human, professional, and memorable. It is stronger than generic SaaS names because it connects emotionally with African service businesses while still sounding modern enough for a serious web platform.

### Brand Interpretation

**Servana means: “service operations made clear, trusted, and manageable for African SMEs.”**

It should not feel like a cold admin system. It should feel like a dependable operating partner for salons, barbershops, spas, massage parlours, grooming studios, and beauty parlours.

# 8. Tone of Voice

## Voice Principles

### Clear

Avoid vague SaaS buzzwords. Say what the product actually does.

Weak:
“Transform your service ecosystem with intelligent workflows.”

Strong:
“Manage clients, services, invoices, payments, staff commissions, and business records from one dashboard.”

# 10. Visual Identity System

## 10.1 Logo Concept

The attached logo is visually aligned with the Servana concept. It communicates:

* **People** through the three abstract human figures
* **Service and support** through the brown hand-like shape holding the figures
* **African identity** through the savannah landscape, acacia tree, sun, and wildlife
* **Growth and optimism** through the sunrise/sunset arc
* **Community** through the circular composition
* **Trust and care** through the warm earthy palette

## Logo Meaning

The logo should be interpreted as:

**A community of service businesses supported by one reliable operating platform, rooted in the African market and built to help merchants serve better.**

## Logo Usage

### Primary Logo

Use the full logo with the **Servana** wordmark on:

* website header
* landing pages
* business proposals
* pitch decks
* invoices
* email signatures
* LinkedIn company profile
* product brochures

### Icon/Favicon

Use the symbol only on:

* favicon
* mobile app icon
* browser tab
* dashboard sidebar collapsed state
* social media profile image
* system loading screen
* small watermark

### Logo Clear Space

Use the height of the letter **“S”** in the wordmark as the minimum clear space around the full logo.

### Minimum Sizes

Full logo:

* Digital minimum width: **180 px**
* Print minimum width: **40 mm**

Icon only:

* Digital minimum: **32 × 32 px**
* Favicon: simplified icon at **16 × 16, 32 × 32, 48 × 48 px**

At very small sizes, the tiny animals and birds will likely lose detail. For favicon use, the people, tree, sun, and hand should be preserved while tiny wildlife details can be simplified.

Likelihood that the full detailed icon remains clear at 16×16 without simplification: **25%**.
Likelihood that a simplified icon version remains clear at 16×16: **85%**.

# 12. Typography System

## Recommended Primary Typeface

Best for SaaS UI because it is clean, highly readable, modern, and professional.

Use for:

* dashboard UI
* tables
* forms
* navigation
* buttons
* reports
* invoices
* admin screens

## Recommended Brand / Marketing Typeface

# **Nunito Sans**

Best for adding warmth and friendliness without becoming childish.

Use for:

* website headings
* marketing banners
* social posts
* explainer sections
* onboarding screens

## Alternative Pairing

Headings: **Manrope**
Body/UI: **Inter**

This pairing feels more premium and modern.

## Typography Hierarchy

### H1

Font: Nunito Sans / Manrope
Weight: 700–800
Use for major page titles.

Example:
**Run your service business with clarity.**

### H2

Weight: 700
Use for section headings.

Example:
**Everything your front office needs in one dashboard.**

### H3

Weight: 600
Use for feature titles and dashboard modules.

Example:
**Client Records**

### Body Text

Font: Inter
Weight: 400–500
Use for descriptions, UI copy, help text, and normal content.

### Buttons

Font: Inter
Weight: 600
Use sentence case.

Good: **Create invoice**
Avoid: **CREATE INVOICE**

# 15. Brand Storytelling

## Core Brand Story

Across Africa, many service-based SMEs are built on hard work, loyal clients, skilled hands, and daily human interaction. But behind the counter, operations are often scattered: client records in notebooks, service histories in memory, payments confirmed manually, commissions calculated after disputes, and business records difficult to audit.

**Servana exists to bring order to that reality.**

It gives service businesses a warm, simple, and secure way to manage daily operations without losing the human side of service. From the front desk to the service chair, from the merchant owner to the platform administrator, Servana helps every role work with more clarity, trust, and accountability.

## Story Theme

# **From scattered daily work to trusted service operations.**

## Emotional Promise

**Servana helps businesses feel more in control.**

## Rational Promise

**Servana centralizes operational records, client activity, invoices, payment records, commissions, fees, permissions, and audit trails.**

## Brand Narrative Paragraph

**Servana was created for the service businesses that keep African communities moving: barbershops, salons, spas, grooming studios, beauty parlours, massage parlours, and similar SMEs. These businesses serve people every day, but their operations often depend on scattered records, manual payment confirmations, and informal staff tracking. Servana gives them one secure dashboard to manage clients, services, invoices, payments, commissions, permissions, platform fees, and audit-ready records—so they can serve better, run smarter, and grow steadily.**

# 20. Do’s and Don’ts

## Do

Use warm, human, simple language.

Use the logo consistently.

Use orange for primary action.

Use teal and green to support operational clarity.

Use real business examples.

Speak directly to merchants and staff.

Emphasize trust, records, service flow, and accountability.

## Don’t

Do not describe Servana as only a booking app.

Do not overuse generic SaaS phrases like “revolutionary ecosystem.”

Do not make the brand too playful.

Do not over-decorate the admin UI.

Do not use random African patterns without meaning.

Do not use tiny logo details in favicon sizes.

Do not make the product sound like it is only for beauty salons; keep it broader: service-based SMEs.
