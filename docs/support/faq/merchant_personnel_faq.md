Servana helps service businesses run each branch day with clarity, speed, and trust.

The Merchant Personnel account is built for the service providers who deliver services to clients. It helps personnel view their own assignments, queue, appointments, service history, commission visibility, preferred personnel requests, and allowed client details needed to complete assigned work.

# 1. About Servana

## 1.1 What is Servana?

Servana is a service-operations SaaS platform for African service-based SMEs.

It helps merchants manage clients, staff, services, queues, appointments, invoices, offline payment records, receipts, commissions, platform fees, reports, and audit-ready records from one secure web dashboard.

Servana is not only a booking app, POS system, staff app, or invoicing tool. It is a practical operating system for service businesses.

---

## 1.2 Who is Servana built for?

Servana is built for service-based SMEs such as:

* Barbershops
* Salons
* Spas
* Massage parlours
* Grooming studios
* Beauty parlours
* Similar appointment or walk-in service businesses

It is especially useful where clients choose services, staff deliver those services, Front Office manages client flow, Finance validates payments, and the business needs clear service and commission records.

---

## 1.3 What does Servana help personnel do?

The Merchant Personnel account helps personnel:

* View their own dashboard
* View their own assignments
* View their own queue
* View their own appointments
* View their own service history
* View their own commission visibility
* View preferred personnel requests
* View clients who specifically requested them
* View estimated wait order
* View services requested
* View whether a request is active, cancelled, reassigned, or completed
* View allowed personally served clients without export capability
* Work from a mobile-friendly interface

The account is designed to help service providers focus on service delivery.

---

## 1.4 Does the Personnel account manage the whole branch?

No. The Merchant Personnel account is not a branch management role.

Personnel users see only the records they are allowed to see, such as their own assignments, queue, appointments, service history, commission visibility, and allowed personally served clients.

Branch setup, service configuration, staff assignment, queue configuration, payment validation, and audit oversight are handled by other roles.

---

## 1.5 Does the Personnel account process payments?

No. Servana records offline payments, but Personnel users do not process client payments inside Servana.

Payment recording is normally handled by Front Office or another authorized role. Payment validation is handled by Finance where validation is enabled.

# 2. Merchant Personnel Account Basics

## 2.1 What is the Merchant Personnel account?

The Merchant Personnel account is the Servana role used by service providers.

It helps personnel see the work assigned to them, understand who is waiting, manage their active service flow where enabled, review service history, and view commission-related information where the merchant allows it.

---

## 2.2 Who should use the Merchant Personnel account?

This account should be used by staff members who directly provide services to clients.

Examples include:

* Barber
* Hairdresser
* Stylist
* Massage therapist
* Nail technician
* Beautician
* Facial therapist
* Grooming specialist
* Cosmetologist
* Other service provider roles configured by the merchant

---

## 2.3 What is the main purpose of this account?

The main purpose is to help service providers know what they need to do next.

The Merchant Personnel account helps answer practical questions such as:

* Who is assigned to me?
* Who is waiting for me?
* Which appointment is next?
* Which service was requested?
* Which client specifically requested me?
* What is the estimated wait order?
* Which requests are active?
* Which requests were cancelled?
* Which requests were reassigned?
* Which services have I completed?
* What commission visibility is available to me?
* Which clients may I view because I personally served them or was assigned to them?

---

## 2.4 Is the Personnel account branch-specific?

Yes. A Personnel user should only see records for the branch or branches they have been assigned to.

If a personnel member works across multiple branches, the merchant must assign that access clearly.

---

## 2.5 Can Personnel users see another merchant’s data?

No.

Each merchant operates as a separate tenant in Servana. A Personnel user should only access records connected to their assigned merchant and branch scope.

---

## 2.6 Can Personnel users see all clients in the branch?

No. Personnel users should not see all clients by default.

They may view only the client information needed for assigned or personally served clients, depending on permissions and branch workflow.

---

## 2.7 Can Personnel users export client contacts?

No. Merchant Personnel users should not have a client contact export function, bulk contact download function, or contact export field.

Personnel may view only the client information needed to complete allowed service work.

# 3. Access and Login

## 3.1 How does a Merchant Personnel user log in?

Merchant Personnel users log in through a Magic Link sent to their email address.

The user requests access, receives a login link, and clicks the link to enter Servana.

---

## 3.2 Can a Personnel user log in just because their email exists?

No. Servana checks more than the email address.

Before access is allowed, the platform checks that:

* The email exists.
* The user belongs to the correct merchant tenant.
* The user account is active.
* The Personnel role is active.
* The user has not been suspended.
* The user is assigned to the correct branch where branch access applies.
* The Magic Link is valid, unused, and not expired.

This helps protect client records, branch assignments, and staff activity.

---

## 3.3 What should I do if my Magic Link does not work?

Request a new Magic Link first.

A Magic Link may fail because:

* It expired.
* It was already used.
* It was opened from the wrong browser or device.
* You are checking the wrong email inbox.
* Your account is inactive.
* Your role is inactive.
* Your account has been suspended.
* Your branch assignment is missing or inactive.
* Your browser or email client blocked the login flow.

If the issue continues, use the approved Help & Support workflow.

---

## 3.4 Can I share my Magic Link with another staff member?

No. Magic Links should not be shared.

Each user should access Servana through their own account. Sharing access makes service history, queue activity, appointment activity, and commission visibility unclear.

---

## 3.5 What should happen when a Personnel user leaves the business?

The appropriate HR user or administrator should suspend or deactivate the user’s access.

Past service records, assignment records, commission-related records, and audit activity should remain available for business continuity and accountability.

The Personnel dashboard is the main screen for the service provider.

The dashboard may show:

They should not see unrelated branch-wide records unless the merchant intentionally grants another role or permission.

## 4.5 What does “next assigned client” mean?

The next assigned client is the client the Personnel user is expected to serve next.

This may come from the queue, an appointment, or a preferred personnel request.

# 5. Own Assignments

## 5.1 What is an assignment?

An assignment connects a Personnel user to a client service request.

It may include:

* Client reference
* Branch
* Service requested
* Queue entry
* Appointment record
* Preferred personnel request
* Estimated wait order
* Service status
* Notes where available
* Start or completion information

---

## 5.2 Can Personnel users see all assignments in the branch?

No. Personnel users should see their own assignments only.

They should not see another personnel member’s full workload unless another authorized workflow gives them that visibility.

---

## 5.3 What assignment details should Personnel see?

Personnel may see:

* Client name or allowed client reference
* Service requested
* Appointment time where applicable
* Queue position
* Estimated wait order
* Request type
* Preferred personnel flag where applicable
* Status
* Branch
* Relevant service notes where available

The information shown should be enough to complete the assigned service.

---

## 5.4 Can Personnel assign clients to themselves?

Usually no.

Client assignment is normally handled through Front Office, Branch, queue rules, appointment workflow, or preferred personnel selection.

Personnel should not bypass the branch assignment process unless a configured workflow allows it.

---

## 5.5 Can Personnel reassign a client to another staff member?

Usually no.

Operational reassignment should be handled by the Branch account user or another authorized role. This protects queue order, appointment flow, preferred personnel rules, and service records.

---

## 5.6 What should Personnel do if an assignment looks wrong?

The Personnel user should notify the correct branch user, Front Office user, or manager.

Examples of assignment issues include:

* Wrong service
* Wrong client
* Wrong personnel
* Duplicate assignment
* Client already served
* Client cancelled
* Client requested another personnel
* Personnel not eligible for the selected service
* Appointment time conflict

# 6. Own Queue

## 6.1 What is the Personnel queue?

The Personnel queue is the list of clients assigned to that Personnel user.

It helps the service provider know who is waiting, who is next, and which service each client requested.

---

## 6.2 Can Personnel see the whole branch queue?

Not by default.

Personnel users should see their own queue and allowed assignment information. Branch-wide queue control belongs to the Branch or Front Office workflow.

---

## 6.3 What queue statuses should Personnel understand?

Useful queue statuses include:

* Waiting
* Assigned
* In service
* Completed
* Cancelled
* No-show

The exact labels may depend on branch configuration.

---

## 6.4 What does Waiting mean?

Waiting means the client is waiting to be served.

The client may be assigned to the Personnel user or waiting for the Personnel user to become available.

---

## 6.5 What does Assigned mean?

Assigned means the client has been assigned to the Personnel user but the service has not started yet.

---

## 6.6 What does In Service mean?

In Service means the Personnel user is currently serving the client.

---

## 6.7 What does Completed mean?

Completed means the service has been finished.

After completion, Front Office or another role may continue with invoice, payment recording, Finance validation, and receipt workflows.

---

## 6.8 What does Cancelled mean?

Cancelled means the service flow was stopped before completion.

A cancellation may happen because the client left, changed service, rescheduled, or was reassigned.

---

## 6.9 What does No-show mean?

No-show means the client did not arrive or did not respond when expected.

This is common for appointments.

---

## 6.10 Can Personnel reorder the queue?

Usually no.

Queue order is controlled by branch queue settings, appointment rules, Front Office actions, Branch actions, and preferred personnel rules.

---

## 6.11 Why is queue order important?

Queue order affects client waiting time, staff workload, preferred personnel commitments, and branch fairness.

Changing queue order without the correct workflow can confuse clients and staff.

---

# 11. Preferred Personnel Requests

## 11.1 What is a preferred personnel request?

A preferred personnel request happens when a client asks to wait for a specific service provider instead of taking the next available personnel.

This is common in barbershops, salons, spas, massage parlours, grooming studios, and beauty businesses.

---

## 11.2 Can Personnel see clients who specifically requested them?

Yes. Personnel users can view preferred personnel requests assigned to them.

This helps them understand which clients are waiting specifically for them.

---

## 11.3 Can preferred personnel requests include an extra fee?

Yes, where enabled.

The preferred personnel fee is configured at platform level and shown to the client before confirmation. It may appear as a separate invoice and receipt line item.

---

## 11.4 Does Personnel choose the preferred personnel fee?

No. Personnel users do not configure the preferred personnel fee.

The fee is controlled through the platform and merchant setup.

---

## 11.5 Can Personnel accept or reject preferred requests?

This depends on branch configuration.

Some branches may allow Personnel to view only. Others may allow limited status updates. Reassignment, cancellation, fee reversal, or invoice adjustment should follow the branch workflow.

---

## 11.6 What happens if Personnel becomes unavailable after a client requested them?

The branch may need to handle one of the following:

* Client waits longer
* Client is reassigned to another eligible personnel
* Client cancels
* Preferred fee is reversed where appropriate
* Invoice is adjusted where appropriate

The action should be recorded.

---

## 11.7 What should Personnel do if they are unavailable for a preferred request?

Personnel should update their permitted availability state where enabled and notify the correct Branch, Front Office, or HR user immediately.

The branch can then decide how to handle the client request.

---

# 14. Service Eligibility

## 14.1 What is service eligibility?

Service eligibility defines which services a Personnel user is allowed to perform.

For example, one staff member may be eligible for haircuts, another for massage services, another for nails, and another for facials.

---

## 14.2 Who manages service eligibility?

Service eligibility is controlled through the HR workflow.

Personnel users do not assign service eligibility to themselves.

---

## 14.3 Why does service eligibility matter?

Service eligibility helps the branch assign the right person to the right service.

It helps prevent clients from being assigned to a staff member who is not approved to perform the selected service.

---

## 14.4 Can Front Office assign Personnel to a service they are not eligible for?

No. The system should use HR-controlled service eligibility when showing available Personnel.

---

## 14.5 What should Personnel do if they are not receiving a service they can perform?

Personnel should notify HR, the Branch user, or a manager.

The issue may be caused by missing service eligibility, inactive branch assignment, availability status, or service configuration.

---

## 14.6 Can Personnel update their own service eligibility?

No. Service eligibility should be managed by HR or another authorized merchant role.

Availability shows whether Personnel can receive assignments or serve clients.

## 15.2 What availability states can exist?

Useful Personnel availability states include:

* Available
* Busy
* On break
* Offline
* Unavailable
* Suspended

---

## 15.3 Who controls availability?

HR and the Merchant Administrator control permanent availability, branch assignment, and staff status.

Personnel may be allowed to toggle limited operational states where the merchant allows it, such as on break or temporarily unavailable.

---

## 15.4 What does Available mean?

Available means the Personnel user can receive assignments according to branch rules, service eligibility, and queue or appointment settings.

---

## 15.5 What does Busy mean?

Busy means the Personnel user is currently serving a client or is not ready for a new assignment.

---

## 15.6 What does On Break mean?

On Break means the Personnel user is temporarily unavailable because they are on a scheduled or operational break.

---

## 15.7 What does Offline mean?

Offline means the Personnel user is not currently active in the platform or not available for assignment.

---

## 15.8 What does Unavailable mean?

Unavailable means the Personnel user cannot currently serve clients.

This may be used for sickness, emergency, temporary absence, or another branch reason.

---

## 15.9 What does Suspended mean?

Suspended means the user’s account access is blocked.

A suspended user should not be able to log in or receive active assignments.

---

## 15.10 Why should Personnel keep their working state accurate?

Accurate working state helps the branch manage:

* Client wait times
* Queue assignment
* Appointment assignment
* Preferred personnel requests
* Service-session flow
* Branch reports
* Staff performance visibility
* Client communication

Wrong availability can create delays and confusion.

# 16. Service Flow

## 16.1 What is the typical Personnel service flow?

A practical service flow may look like this:

1. Client is registered or retrieved by Front Office.
2. Client chooses a service.
3. Client is placed into a queue or appointment flow.
4. Client is assigned to eligible Personnel.
5. Personnel views the assignment.
6. Service starts.
7. Service is completed.
8. Invoice is generated by the configured role.
9. Offline payment is recorded.
10. Finance validates payment where enabled.
11. Receipt is generated where rules are satisfied.
12. Service history and commission visibility update where applicable.

---

## 16.2 Does Personnel control the whole service flow?

No. Personnel controls only the parts assigned to them or enabled for their role.

Front Office, Branch, HR, Finance, Audit, and Merchant Administrator roles each manage different parts of the flow.

---

## 16.3 Why does Servana separate these roles?

Role separation keeps the branch organized.

It helps make sure:

* HR controls staff readiness.
* Front Office manages client flow.
* Personnel provide services.
* Finance validates payments.
* Audit reviews activity.
* Branch users manage branch operations.
* Merchant Administrators oversee the merchant account.

---

## 16.4 What should Personnel do after completing a service?

Personnel should follow the branch workflow.

This may include marking the service complete, adding allowed notes, notifying Front Office, or waiting for the invoice and payment steps to continue through other roles.

---

# 18. Working With Other Roles

## 18.1 How does Personnel work with Front Office?

Front Office manages client-facing branch flow.

Front Office may register clients, check appointments, create walk-ins, place clients in queues, assign eligible Personnel, create invoices, and record offline payment details.

Personnel receives and completes assigned service work.

---

## 18.2 How does Personnel work with HR?

HR manages staff profiles, branch assignment, service eligibility, availability, and staff status.

Personnel should contact HR or the appropriate manager when service eligibility, availability, profile details, or account status needs review.

---

## 18.3 How does Personnel work with the Branch account user?

The Branch account user manages branch operations, services, hours, queue settings, appointment controls, day opening, day closing, and cash-up submission.

Personnel follows the branch flow and serves assigned clients.

---

## 18.4 How does Personnel work with Finance?

Finance validates payments and may review commission-related financial records.

Personnel may see commission visibility where enabled, but Finance controls validation-related payment and receipt steps.

---

## 18.5 How does Personnel work with Audit?

Audit reviews activity after it happens.

Personnel actions, such as service completion, availability changes, or unauthorized access attempts, may appear in audit activity where enabled.

---

## 18.6 How does Personnel work with the Merchant Administrator?

The Merchant Administrator oversees the merchant account and may view branch performance, staff performance, service performance, and platform fee records.

Personnel helps the merchant by keeping service delivery accurate and timely.

# 19. Common Personnel Scenarios

## 19.1 A client is waiting for me. What should I do?

Open your Personnel dashboard and check your queue or assignments.

Confirm:

* Client reference
* Service requested
* Queue position
* Preferred request status, if any
* Appointment time, if any
* Whether you are available to start

Then follow the branch workflow for starting the service.

---

## 19.2 I cannot see a client I expected. What should I check?

Check whether:

* The client is assigned to you.
* The client is in the correct branch.
* The appointment has been checked in.
* The queue entry is active.
* The client was reassigned.
* The request was cancelled.
* Your availability status is correct.
* Your service eligibility is active.
* Your account is assigned to the right branch.

If the issue continues, notify Front Office, Branch, or HR.

---

## 19.3 I am assigned to a service I do not perform. What should I do?

Notify Front Office, HR, or the Branch user immediately.

Do not continue the service if you are not eligible or prepared to perform it. HR may need to review service eligibility or the assignment may need correction.

---

## 19.4 I need to go on break. What should I do?

Use the allowed availability control if enabled.

Also notify the branch team if clients are waiting, if appointments are coming up, or if preferred personnel requests are active.

---

## 19.5 I am unavailable for an appointment. What should I do?

Notify the correct Branch, Front Office, or HR user as soon as possible.

The branch may need to reassign, reschedule, cancel, or ask the client to wait.

---

## 19.6 A client requested me but I am busy. What should I do?

Check your queue and estimated wait order.

If the client is willing to wait, continue through the branch workflow. If the wait is too long, Front Office or Branch may need to discuss reassignment or cancellation with the client.

---

## 19.7 My commission looks wrong. What should I do?

Raise it through the merchant’s internal process.

Provide:

* Service date
* Service name
* Branch
* Client reference where visible
* Appointment or queue reference where visible
* Commission amount shown
* What looks incorrect

Finance, HR, or the Merchant Administrator may need to review the record.

---

## 19.8 I see a client record I should not see. What should I do?

Report it immediately through the correct internal process or Help & Support workflow.

Do not download, copy, share, or continue viewing information that does not belong to your allowed work.

# 21. Security Basics

## 21.1 What should Personnel users do to keep access secure?

Personnel users should:

* Use their own email account.
* Never share Magic Links.
* Keep email access secure.
* Log out on shared devices.
* Avoid shared browsers where possible.
* Check that assignments belong to the correct branch.
* Avoid unnecessary screenshots.
* Avoid copying client information outside Servana.
* Report suspicious activity quickly.
* Keep client and branch information confidential within the business.

---

## 21.2 Why is account sharing a problem?

Account sharing makes service records unclear.

If several people use one Personnel account, the business cannot confidently tell who served a client, started a service, completed a service, viewed a client record, or changed availability.

Each user should use their own account.

---

## 21.3 What should Personnel do if suspicious activity appears?

Personnel should:

1. Stop and review the affected record.
2. Note the client, branch, time, and module where relevant.
3. Notify Front Office, Branch, HR, or the Merchant Administrator.
4. Use the Help & Support workflow if platform support is needed.

---

## 21.4 What should happen after Personnel access is suspended?

The Personnel user should no longer be able to log in.

Active sessions and unused Magic Links should be invalidated. Historical records connected to that user should remain visible.
