## Welcome to Servana

Servana helps service businesses run with clarity, trust, and better daily control.

The Merchant Human Resource account is built for the people who manage staff access and staff readiness inside a specific branch. It helps HR users invite staff, create staff profiles, assign branch-scoped roles, manage service eligibility, maintain availability, update employment details, suspend or deactivate staff access, and keep staff records organized.

This FAQ explains how the Merchant Human Resource account works in clear, practical language. It is a Help document, not a legal document.

For full details on platform use, data handling, privacy, and customer responsibilities, please refer to Servana’s **Terms of Service**, **Data Policy**, and **Privacy Policy**.

# 1. About Servana

## 1.1 What is Servana?

Servana is a service-operations SaaS platform for African service-based SMEs.

It helps merchants manage clients, staff, branches, services, queues, appointments, invoices, offline payment records, receipts, commissions, platform fees, permissions, reports, and audit-ready records from one secure web dashboard.

Servana is not only a booking app, POS system, HR tool, or invoicing tool. It is a practical operating system for service businesses.

---

## 1.2 Who is Servana built for?

Servana is built for service-based SMEs such as:

* Barbershops
* Salons
* Spas
* Massage parlours
* Grooming studios
* Beauty parlours
* Similar appointment or walk-in service businesses

It is especially useful for businesses where staff roles, service eligibility, branch assignments, availability, queues, appointments, commissions, and client-service records need to stay organized.

---

## 1.3 Why does Servana include an HR account?

Servana includes an HR account because service businesses depend on people.

A branch needs to know:

* Which staff members are active.
* Which staff members are assigned to the branch.
* Which services each personnel member can perform.
* Which staff members are available today.
* Which staff members are unavailable, suspended, or deactivated.
* Which users have access to Front Office, Finance, Personnel, or Audit tools.
* Which invitations are pending.
* Which staff changes were made and by whom.

The Merchant Human Resource account helps keep those people-operations records clear.

---

## 1.4 Does the HR account manage all staff across the merchant?

No. The Merchant Human Resource account is branch-scoped.

An HR user manages staff only within the same branch they are assigned to. The HR user should not manage staff in other branches unless the correct branch access has been intentionally assigned.

# 2. Merchant Human Resource Account Basics

## 2.1 What is the Merchant Human Resource account?

The Merchant Human Resource account is the Servana role used to manage staff identity, branch-scoped staff assignment, role assignment, service eligibility, staff availability, staff profile details, invitations, suspension, deactivation, and staff lifecycle visibility.

It helps the branch keep staff access and operational readiness organized.

---

## 2.2 Who should use the Merchant Human Resource account?

This account should be used by a trusted HR or staff operations user.

Typical users may include:

* Branch HR user
* Staff coordinator
* Operations supervisor
* Branch administrator
* Personnel scheduler
* Service team coordinator
* Business owner acting in an HR role
* Authorized staff records manager

The user should be trusted because HR actions affect who can access the platform and what they can do inside the branch.

---

## 2.3 What is the main purpose of this account?

The main purpose is to keep branch staff records and access clear.

The Merchant Human Resource account helps answer practical questions such as:

* Who works in this branch?
* Who has been invited but has not yet activated access?
* Which staff members are active?
* Which staff members are suspended or deactivated?
* Which staff member has which role?
* Which services can each personnel member perform?
* Who is available today?
* Who is on break, unavailable, off-duty, or suspended?
* Which staff records need updating?
* Who changed a staff role, status, availability, or service eligibility?

---

## 2.4 Is the HR account the same as the Merchant Administrator account?

No.

The Merchant Administrator owns and oversees the merchant account. The Merchant Human Resource account manages staff within its assigned branch scope.

The HR user does not replace the Merchant Administrator.

---

## 2.5 Is the HR account the same as the Branch account?

No.

The Branch account manages the branch setup and operational settings, such as services, pricing, operating hours, queue configuration, appointment controls, day opening, day closing, and cash-up submission.

The HR account manages staff identity, roles, branch assignment, availability, and service eligibility inside the branch.

---

## 2.6 Is the HR account the same as Front Office?

No.

Front Office handles client-facing activity, such as client lookup, walk-ins, appointments, queues, invoices, and payment recording.

HR controls which staff members exist, what role they hold, and which services personnel can perform.

---

## 2.7 Is the HR account the same as Finance?

No.

Finance validates payments, reviews cash-up, manages finance records, receipts, disputes, refunds, and finance reports.

HR manages staff records and staff access.

# 3. Access and Login

## 3.1 How does a Merchant Human Resource user log in?

Merchant Human Resource users log in through a Magic Link sent to their email address.

The user requests access, receives a login link, and clicks the link to enter Servana.

---

## 3.2 Can an HR user log in just because their email exists?

No. Servana checks more than the email address.

Before access is allowed, the platform checks that:

* The email exists.
* The user belongs to the correct merchant tenant.
* The user account is active.
* The HR role is active.
* The user has not been suspended.
* The user is assigned to the correct branch.
* The Magic Link is valid, unused, and not expired.

This helps protect staff records and branch access controls.

---

## 3.3 What should I do if my Magic Link does not work?

Request a new Magic Link first.

A Magic Link may fail because:

* It expired.
* It was already used.
* It was opened from the wrong browser or device.
* You are checking the wrong email inbox.
* Your account is inactive.
* Your role is inactive.
* Your account has been suspended.
* Your branch assignment is missing or inactive.
* Your browser or email client blocked the login flow.

If the issue continues, use the approved Help & Support workflow.

---

## 3.4 Can I share my Magic Link with another user?

No. Magic Links should not be shared.

Each user should access Servana through their own account. Shared access makes staff activity records unclear and weakens accountability.

---

## 3.5 What should happen when an HR user leaves the business?

The appropriate administrator should suspend, deactivate, or remove the user’s access promptly.

Past activity connected to that HR user should remain visible so the merchant can still understand staff invitations, role assignments, profile changes, availability changes, service eligibility updates, suspensions, and deactivations.

---

# 4. HR Dashboard

## 4.1 What is the HR dashboard?

The HR dashboard is the main screen for staff and access management inside the HR user’s assigned branch.

It helps the HR user see staff status, pending invitations, availability issues, and staff records that need attention.

---

## 4.2 What should the HR dashboard show?

The HR dashboard may show:

* Total staff count
* Active staff count
* Suspended staff count
* Deactivated staff count
* Pending invitations
* Revoked invitations
* Staff awaiting activation
* Unavailable personnel
* Personnel on break
* Personnel with missing service eligibility
* Staff with incomplete profiles
* Recent staff changes
* Branch filter, where applicable
* HR-visible activity logs

The dashboard should help the HR user understand staff readiness at a glance.

---

## 4.3 Why does the HR dashboard focus on staff readiness?

A branch cannot run smoothly if staff access, availability, or service eligibility is unclear.

The HR dashboard helps the branch avoid situations where:

* A staff member cannot log in.
* A staff member is assigned the wrong role.
* A personnel member cannot receive service assignments.
* A suspended user still appears active.
* A staff member is unavailable but still receiving clients.
* Front Office cannot see eligible personnel for a service.

---

## 4.4 What does “pending invitation” mean?

Pending invitation means the staff member has been invited but has not yet completed the activation process.

The staff member may still need to open the welcome email or log in through the Magic Link flow.

---

## 4.5 What does “active staff” mean?

Active staff means the staff member’s account is enabled and can be used according to the assigned role, branch, and permissions.

---

## 4.6 What does “suspended staff” mean?

Suspended staff means the staff member’s access is blocked, but historical records connected to the staff member remain preserved.

Suspension is useful when access should stop immediately without losing past records.

---

## 4.7 What does “deactivated staff” mean?

Deactivated staff means the staff member is no longer active for ongoing platform use.

Historical operational records should remain available for reporting, audit, and business continuity.

---

# 5. Staff Invitation and Activation

## 5.1 Can HR invite staff?

Yes. Merchant Human Resource users can invite staff within their assigned branch scope.

The HR user should add the staff email, select the account type, select the staff role, complete required profile details, and trigger the invitation.

---

## 5.2 Which staff account types can HR create?

The HR user can create staff users for the permitted branch account types, such as:

* Merchant Personnel
* Merchant Front Office
* Merchant Finance
* Merchant Audit

The exact list depends on platform configuration and merchant permissions.

---

## 5.3 Can HR create Merchant Administrator accounts?

No. The Merchant Human Resource user does not create the Merchant Administrator account.

The Merchant Administrator is created through the merchant self-registration flow and remains a separate account type.

---

## 5.4 Can HR create Merchant Branch accounts?

No. Merchant Branch accounts are created by the Merchant Administrator.

The HR account manages staff users within the assigned branch scope.

---

## 5.5 What steps are involved in inviting staff?

A typical staff invitation flow is:

1. Add the staff member’s email address.
2. Select the staff account type.
3. Select the staff member’s role.
4. Complete the staff profile.
5. Assign the staff member to the correct branch.
6. Assign service eligibility if the staff member is Merchant Personnel.
7. Review the permission preview.
8. Send the invitation.
9. Track pending activation.
10. Resend or revoke the invite where needed.

---

## 5.6 What does the staff member receive after invitation?

The staff member should receive a welcome email explaining how to log in through Magic Link.

The email should help the staff member understand that access is tied to the invited email address, assigned merchant, branch, role, and account status.

---

## 5.7 Can HR resend an invitation?

Yes. HR can resend an invitation where the feature is enabled.

This is useful if the staff member did not receive the email, deleted it, or failed to activate access before the invitation expired.

---

## 5.8 Can HR revoke an invitation?

Yes. HR can revoke an invitation where the feature is enabled.

Revoking an invitation is useful if the wrong email was entered, the wrong role was selected, or the staff member should no longer receive access.

---

## 5.9 What should HR check before sending an invitation?

Before sending an invitation, HR should check:

* Email address
* Phone number
* First name
* Last name
* Display name
* Branch
* Account type
* Role
* Employment status
* Service eligibility, where applicable
* Permission preview
* Whether a duplicate staff record already exists

A careful invitation prevents access problems later.

---

A staff profile is the staff member’s record inside Servana.

It helps the branch identify the staff member, understand their role, manage access, track availability, and connect staff activity to branch operations.

---

## 6.2 What information should a staff profile include?

A staff profile should include:

* Last name
* Display name
* Profile picture, where configured by merchant policy
* Email
* Phone number
* Role
* Employment type
* Employment status
* Primary branch
* Start date
* Staff invited by

* Status history
* Role history
* Branch history

---

## 6.3 Why is the display name important?

The display name is the name shown across the branch workflow.

It may appear in staff rosters, personnel lists, appointment assignment, queue assignment, reports, and service history. It should be clear and recognizable.

---

## 6.4 Why should email and phone be unique?

Email and phone uniqueness helps prevent duplicate staff accounts.

Duplicate staff records can cause access issues, wrong assignments, unclear commission records, and confused reports.

---

## 6.5 Can HR edit staff profiles?

Yes. HR can edit staff profiles within the assigned branch scope where permission allows it.

Profile changes should be accurate because they affect staff visibility, access, reports, and operational workflows.

---

## 6.6 What should HR check before saving profile changes?

Check:

* Correct staff member
* Correct branch
* Correct role
* Correct employment status
* Correct email and phone
* Correct service eligibility
* Correct availability settings
* Whether the change affects live appointments, queues, or service sessions

# 7. Staff Roles and Account Types

## 7.1 What is the difference between account type and role?

The account type determines the main area of Servana the staff member uses.

Examples:

* Personnel
* Front Office
* Finance
* Audit

The role describes the staff member’s practical job or function within that account type.

Examples:

* Barber
* Stylist
* Beautician
* Massage Therapist
* Receptionist
* Finance Reviewer
* Audit Reviewer

---

## 7.2 What is a Merchant Personnel user?

Merchant Personnel users are service providers.

Examples include:

* Barber
* Hairdresser
* Stylist
* Massage therapist
* Nail technician
* Beautician
* Facial therapist
* Grooming specialist
* Cosmetologist

Personnel users can view their own assignments, queue, appointments, service history, commission visibility, and allowed personally served clients.

---

## 7.3 What is a Merchant Front Office user?

Merchant Front Office users handle client-facing branch operations.

They may register clients, retrieve existing clients, create walk-ins, create appointments, check in clients, manage queue activity, select services, assign eligible personnel, generate invoices, and record offline payment details.

---

## 7.4 What is a Merchant Finance user?

Merchant Finance users handle finance workflows.

They may view invoices, validate offline payments, approve or reject payment records, generate receipts after validation, review cash-up records, handle payment disputes, view platform fee records where permitted, and review finance reports.

---

## 7.5 What is a Merchant Audit user?

Merchant Audit users provide read-only oversight.

They may review audit logs, role changes, branch changes, invoice history, payment validation logs, receipt logs, queue reassignment logs, preferred personnel fee logs, and suspicious activity.

---

## 7.6 Can HR assign any role to any user?

No. HR should assign roles that match the staff member’s actual branch function.

High-risk roles should be assigned carefully because they may affect client records, payments, receipts, exports, audit visibility, or staff access.

---

## 7.7 Can HR assign itself a higher-risk role?

No. HR users should not self-escalate permissions.

If an HR user needs a different role or higher access, the correct administrator or approved workflow should handle it.

---

## 7.8 Why should HR use permission preview?

Permission preview helps HR understand what the staff member will be able to see and do before the role is saved.

This reduces accidental over-permissioning.

---

# 8. Branch Scope

## 8.1 What does branch scope mean?

Branch scope means the HR user can manage staff only within the branch they are assigned to.

This keeps staff records and access organized by business location.

---

## 8.2 Can HR manage staff in other branches?

No. HR users should not manage staff in other branches unless the correct access has been intentionally assigned.

---

## 8.3 Can HR assign staff to another branch?

No. A Merchant Human Resource user should assign staff only within the same branch scope.

Cross-branch staff assignment should follow the correct merchant workflow.

---

## 8.4 Why is branch scope important?

Branch scope helps protect:

* Staff records
* Client records
* Service eligibility
* Queue assignment
* Appointment assignment
* Finance access
* Audit visibility
* Branch reports

It prevents one branch from accidentally managing another branch’s users.

---

## 8.5 What should HR do if a staff member needs access to another branch?

HR should escalate the request through the appropriate merchant-side process.

The request should be handled carefully because multi-branch access affects operational visibility and record access.

---

# 9. Service Eligibility

## 9.1 What is service eligibility?

Service eligibility defines which services a Merchant Personnel user is allowed to perform.

For example, one staff member may be eligible for haircuts, while another may be eligible for massage services, facials, nail services, or grooming services.

---

## 9.2 Who manages service eligibility?

The Merchant Human Resource user manages personnel service eligibility within the assigned branch scope.

---

## 9.3 Why does HR control service eligibility?

HR controls service eligibility because it is connected to staff skills, role suitability, branch assignment, and operational readiness.

This prevents Front Office from assigning a client to someone who is not approved to perform the selected service.

---

## 9.4 Can Front Office override HR service eligibility?

No. Front Office should not override HR-controlled service eligibility.

Front Office can assign clients only to eligible personnel shown by the system.

---

## 9.5 Can the Branch account user override HR service eligibility?

No. The Branch account user can view HR-controlled personnel information but should not override service eligibility.

---

## 9.6 What happens if service eligibility is missing?

If service eligibility is missing, the personnel member may not appear for assignment to that service.

This can affect appointments, walk-ins, queues, and preferred personnel selection.

---

## 9.7 What should HR check when a service provider is not appearing?

HR should check:

* Staff account status
* Branch assignment
* Role
* Employment status
* Personnel account type
* Service eligibility
* Availability
* Suspension or deactivation status
* Whether the selected service is active for the branch

---

## 9.8 Can service eligibility change over time?

Yes. Service eligibility may change when a staff member gains new skills, changes role, moves branch, stops offering a service, or becomes inactive.

Each change should be recorded clearly.

---

Merchant Human Resource users manage personnel availability within their branch scope.

# 11. Suspension and Deactivation

## 11.1 What is staff suspension?

Suspension blocks a staff member from accessing Servana while preserving historical records.

It is useful when access must stop immediately but past activity should remain visible.

---

## 11.2 What is staff deactivation?

Deactivation marks a staff member as no longer active for ongoing platform use.

It is useful when a staff member has left, changed status, or should no longer appear in active workflows.

---

## 11.3 Can HR suspend staff?

Yes. HR can suspend staff within the assigned branch scope where permission allows it.

Suspension should include a clear reason.

---

## 11.4 Can HR deactivate staff?

Yes. HR can deactivate staff within the assigned branch scope where permission allows it.

Deactivation should be handled carefully because it affects login, assignments, queues, appointments, and service availability.

---

## 11.5 What happens when a staff member is suspended or deactivated?

When a staff member is suspended or deactivated:

* Existing active sessions should be invalidated.
* Unused Magic Links should be invalidated.
* Login should be blocked.
* Historical records should be preserved.
* Live queues, appointments, and service sessions should be checked.
* Reassignment may be needed where the staff member had active work.

---

## 11.6 Why should historical records remain visible?

Historical records help the merchant understand past activity.

A staff member may have created invoices, served clients, recorded actions, handled appointments, or appeared in audit logs. Removing those records would make the business history unclear.

---

## 11.7 What should HR check before suspending or deactivating personnel?

Check whether the staff member has:

* Active queue assignments
* Upcoming appointments
* In-progress service sessions
* Preferred personnel requests
* Pending service work
* Open staff-related issues
* Linked commission records
* Recent access activity

This helps the branch avoid abandoned work.

---

# 13. Duplicate Staff Prevention

## 13.1 Why does Servana prevent duplicate staff records?

Duplicate staff records can create serious confusion.

They can cause:

* Login problems
* Wrong role assignment
* Split service history
* Wrong commission records
* Confused availability
* Duplicate invitations
* Unclear audit history
* Staff appearing twice in assignments

---

## 13.2 How does Servana help prevent duplicates?

Servana should check active staff records by email and phone number.

If a duplicate is detected, the HR user should review the existing record instead of creating another one.

---

## 13.3 What should HR do when a duplicate appears?

HR should not create another staff profile.

The HR user should check whether:

* The staff member already exists.
* The staff member belongs to another branch.
* The email or phone was entered incorrectly.
* The staff member was previously suspended or deactivated.
* The correct workflow is reactivation, transfer, or administrator review.

---

## 13.4 Can inactive staff be reactivated?

This depends on merchant configuration.

Where reactivation is allowed, the HR user should verify the staff member’s branch, role, service eligibility, employment status, and availability before restoring access.

# 17. Common HR Scenarios

## 17.1 A new staff member is joining the branch. What should HR do?

Create the staff profile, assign the correct account type and role, assign the branch, add service eligibility where needed, check the permission preview, and send the invitation.

---

## 17.2 A staff member did not receive the invitation email. What should HR do?

Check that the email address is correct.

If it is correct, resend the invitation where the feature is available. The staff member should also check spam, junk, promotions, or blocked email folders.

---

## 17.3 A staff member was invited with the wrong email. What should HR do?

Revoke the wrong invitation if possible.

Then create or send a new invitation using the correct email address.

---

## 17.4 A personnel member is not showing for a service. What should HR check?

Check:

* Account status
* Branch assignment
* Role
* Employment status
* Availability
* Service eligibility
* Suspension status
* Whether the service is active
* Whether the personnel is eligible for that service

---

## 17.5 A staff member has left the business. What should HR do?

Suspend or deactivate the staff member according to the merchant’s process.

Then check for live assignments, queues, appointments, service sessions, and any reassignment needs.

---

## 17.6 A staff member changed roles. What should HR do?

Update the role carefully.

Before saving, check the permission preview and confirm that the new role matches the staff member’s actual function.

---

## 17.7 A staff member is temporarily unavailable. What should HR do?

Update the staff member’s availability.

If the staff member has live appointments, queues, preferred personnel requests, or in-progress sessions, notify the Branch or Front Office team so they can handle reassignment or client communication.

---

## 17.8 A staff member appears twice in the roster. What should HR do?

Do not create or use a third record.

Check email, phone, branch, status, and activation history. Then follow the merchant’s duplicate-record review process.

---

* Status history
* Staff roster records
* HR-visible audit activity

Exact visibility depends on permissions.

## 18.2 Can HR see client records?

The HR account should not be used for general client record access.

Payment records belong to Finance and other authorized financial workflows.

Yes, where export is enabled.

# 19. Security Basics

## 19.1 What should HR users do to keep access secure?

HR users should:

* Use their own email account.
* Never share Magic Links.
* Keep email access secure.
* Log out on shared devices.
* Avoid shared browsers where possible.
* Review staff roles carefully.
* Avoid unnecessary exports.
* Check that changes are being made in the correct branch.
* Report suspicious activity quickly.
* Keep staff information confidential within the business.

---

## 19.2 Why is account sharing a problem?

Account sharing makes staff-management records unclear.

If several people use one HR account, the business cannot confidently tell who invited staff, changed a role, updated availability, assigned service eligibility, suspended access, or exported the roster.

Each user should use their own account.

---

## 19.3 What should HR do if suspicious staff-access activity appears?

HR should:

1. Review the affected staff record.
2. Check recent role, status, availability, and service eligibility changes.
3. Check who made the change and when.
4. Notify the Merchant Administrator or assigned manager.
5. Use the Help & Support workflow if platform support is needed.

---

## 19.4 What should happen after staff suspension?

The staff member should no longer be able to log in.

Active sessions and unused Magic Links should be invalidated. Historical records connected to the staff member should remain visible.
