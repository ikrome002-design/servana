## **4. Description of Servana and Data-Governance Context**

4.1. Servana is a multi-tenant SaaS web platform owned and operated by Citrus Labs Limited for service-based SMEs, including barbershops, salons, massage parlours, spas, grooming studios, beauty parlours, and similar appointment-based, walk-in, queue-based, or staff-service businesses.

4.2. Servana is not merely a booking system, point-of-sale system, invoice generator, receipt generator, staff tracker, or CRM. It is a service-operations platform intended to support Merchant onboarding, Branch operations, staff access, Client records, walk-ins, appointments, queues, service delivery tracking, invoice generation, offline payment recording, receipt generation, personnel commission visibility, Client data protection, contact access controls, Platform Fee calculation, Merchant financial visibility, reports, dashboards, notifications, and audit-ready operational and financial records.

4.3. Servana operates on a multi-tenant model. Each Merchant operates as an isolated tenant. A Merchant’s Data must not be accessible, inferable, editable, exportable, enumerable, or searchable by another Merchant.

4.4. Servana records offline payments. Client-to-Merchant payments are made outside Servana. Servana may record payment method, amount, reference, payment status, validation status, validation notes, cash-up data, reconciliation records, invoice status, receipt status, and refund records, but Servana does not process Client-to-Merchant payments inside the Platform.

4.5. Supported offline payment methods include cash, M-Pesa, bank transfer, card terminal, voucher, split payment, and other Merchant-defined offline methods.

4.6. Servana includes a Citrus Billing Engine that calculates and tracks what Merchants owe Citrus Labs Limited, including Platform Fees, account-opening fees where applicable, service fee rules, billing cycles, Merchant balances, Branch-level fee debts, overdue balances, fee exemptions, billing audit logs, Merchant-facing statements, and Platform Fee dispute records.

4.7. Servana includes a preferred Merchant Personnel waiting feature, under which a Client may choose to wait for the next available Merchant Personnel user or for a specific preferred Merchant Personnel user at an extra cost configured by the Super Administrator.

4.8. The preferred Merchant Personnel fee may appear as a separate invoice line item, may be treated as Merchant revenue unless configured otherwise, may affect Citrus service-fee treatment according to Platform rules, and may affect personnel commission according to Merchant HR-controlled commission settings.

4.9. Merchant Personnel access is intentionally narrow. Merchant Personnel are not general Merchant users, administrators, Finance users, HR users, Audit users, Branch managers, Client database owners, or contact-export users.

4.10. Merchant Personnel are operational service-provider users whose access is limited to assigned Branches, assigned work, personally served Clients, own commission, own queue, own appointments, own service history, and preferred-personnel requests addressed to them.

## **6. Platform Role Architecture and Governance Model**

6.1. Servana uses role-based, permission-based, tenant-based, Branch-based, status-based, and assignment-based access control.

6.2. Servana’s role architecture is intended to prevent excessive access, unauthorized cross-Branch access, unauthorized cross-Merchant access, improper financial manipulation, Client contact leakage, staff self-escalation, and operational record tampering.

6.3. **Super Administrator.** The Super Administrator is the Citrus Labs Limited platform-owner role. The Super Administrator may configure platform-wide settings, Platform Fee rules, billing cycles, preferred-personnel waiting fees, feature flags, internal Citrus roles, platform-level reports, Platform Fee ledgers, audit logs, merchant suspension, merchant deactivation, billing enforcement, abuse response, and platform-policy enforcement.

6.4. The Super Administrator does not create the first Merchant Administrator, does not create Merchant tenants on behalf of Merchants as a standard workflow, does not require Merchant KYC or compliance approval before dashboard access, and does not perform ordinary Merchant operations except within controlled support, governance, enforcement, legal, or incident workflows.

6.5. **Merchant Administrator.** The Merchant Administrator self-registers the Merchant business account. The system creates the Merchant tenant and assigns the registering user as Merchant Owner / Merchant Administrator. The Merchant Administrator completes first-time setup, selects the Merchant service-fee tier, completes the Merchant profile, creates at least one Branch, adds initial Branch and HR user email addresses, and receives Merchant-level reports.

6.6. **Merchant Branch.** The Merchant Branch user manages only the specific Branch account they have been added into. Branch users may manage Branch profile, operating hours, Branch operating calendar, services, service pricing, Branch service availability, Branch queue configuration, Branch appointment controls, Branch day opening and closing, cash-up submission, Branch reports, Branch invoices, Branch receipts, Branch payment records, and Branch-level audit logs.

6.7. **Merchant Human Resource.** Merchant HR users manage staff identity, staff invitations, role assignment, Branch-scoped account assignment, employment data, personnel service eligibility, availability calendars, working days, working hours, breaks, unavailable dates, emergency unavailability, staff roster searches, staff roster export only, permission preview, status history, and access lifecycle records within their assigned Branch scope.

6.8. **Merchant Finance.** Merchant Finance users manage offline payment validation, payment references, payment approval, payment rejection, invoice payment status, receipt generation after validation, outstanding balances, voided invoices, external refund records, finance disputes, cash-up reviews, period locking where permitted, finance exports where permitted, commission liabilities where permitted, Platform Fee visibility where permitted, and finance audit activity.

6.9. **Merchant Front Office.** Merchant Front Office users manage Client registration, Client retrieval, same-Branch duplicate-client prevention, walk-ins, appointments, check-ins, queues, assignments, service sessions, invoice generation, offline payment recording, Finance-validation submission, operational notifications, and daily Branch activity.

6.10. **Merchant Personnel.** Merchant Personnel are service providers. They may view own dashboard, own assignments, own queue, own appointments, own service history, own commission, preferred-personnel requests, Clients who specifically requested them, estimated wait order, service requested, request state, and allowed personally served Clients. Merchant Personnel must not export Client contacts, bulk download Client data, access other personnel’s records, manage users, validate payments, generate receipts unless separately authorized through a valid payment-state workflow, or perform administrative actions outside their role.

6.11. **Merchant Audit.** Merchant Audit users have read-only oversight of high-risk events, recent activity, flagged items, payment issues, role changes, contact-access or export-attempt events, preferred-personnel overrides, immutable audit logs, invoice history, payment validation logs, receipt logs, queue reassignment logs, and suspicious activity.

6.12. **Client / End-User Records.** Clients initially exist as Client records, with optional future conversion into full login accounts. Client records may include profile details, contact details, visit history, services consumed, assigned personnel history, preferred personnel history, invoice and receipt history, appointment history, queue participation, consent records, and communication preferences.

# **Schedule 3: Offline Payment Governance**

5. Split payments require separate method, amount, and validation status per leg.

6. Partial payments require automatic remaining-balance calculation.

7. Duplicate payment references may be blocked, warned, or escalated.

8. Receipt generation must be blocked before validation.

9. Receipt reissue must reference the original receipt.

10. Receipt reversal must preserve the original record.

11. External refund records must identify refund type, amount, method, reference where applicable, approval status, invoice impact, payment impact, and audit log.
