## 4. DESCRIPTION OF SERVANA

4.1. Servana is a multi-tenant SaaS web platform for service-based SMEs, including barbershops, salons, massage parlours, spas, grooming studios, beauty parlours, and similar appointment-based or walk-in businesses.

4.2. Servana is designed to operate as a service-operations platform through which merchant businesses can digitise, control, and audit day-to-day operations, including merchant onboarding, merchant branches, staff and personnel access, front-office operations, client records, walk-ins, appointments, queue management, service delivery tracking, client choice of next available personnel or preferred personnel, invoice generation, offline payment recording, receipt generation, personnel commission visibility, client data protection, contact access control, platform fee calculation, service fee tier invoice pricing, merchant financial visibility, and audit-ready operational and financial records. 

4.3. Servana must not be treated solely as a booking application, point-of-sale system, payment processor, invoicing tool, staff roster, HR database, or reporting tool. It is a service-operations SaaS platform intended to help Customers run clients, staff, services, queues, invoices, commissions, platform fees, reports, and audit records from one secure web dashboard.

4.4. Servana is structured around tenant isolation. Each merchant operates as an isolated tenant. A merchant’s data must not be accessible, inferable, editable, exportable, or enumerable by another merchant, subject only to controlled Citrus Labs Limited platform-level workflows, lawful support access, security operations, legal compliance, billing enforcement, abuse controls, and other lawful exceptions.

4.5. Servana records offline or off-platform payments. Servana may record payment method, amount, reference, status, validation details, payment notes, partial payment records, split payment records, external refund records, cash-up records, reconciliation records, disputes, receipts, and related audit trails, but Servana does not, by this Policy alone, represent that it directly processes client-to-merchant payments inside the platform.

4.6. Servana includes HR and staff-management functions that permit the Merchant Human Resource user, within the assigned Branch scope and permissions, to add staff users, invite staff by email, resend invitations, revoke invitations, show pending activation, create staff profiles, edit staff profiles, update employment data, assign predefined roles within the HR user’s own Branch scope, assign staff to account types within the same Branch, assign services that Merchant Personnel members can perform, manage personnel service eligibility, manage availability calendars and shifts, manage working days, manage working hours, manage break or off-duty status, manage unavailable dates, manage emergency unavailable status, maintain employment records, maintain role/branch/status history, search and filter staff, export staff roster data only, and view HR-visible audit events limited to staff and access changes.

## 5. MERCHANT HUMAN RESOURCE ROLE AND PRIVACY TREATMENT

5.1. The Merchant Human Resource account is a Customer-side HR and staff-access lifecycle account within Servana. Its purpose is to manage staff identity, employment status, branch-scoped assignment, role assignment, personnel service eligibility, availability, staff invitation, staff activation visibility, staff suspension, staff deactivation, staff records, role history, branch history, status history, and limited staff roster export within the HR user’s assigned Branch scope.

5.2. A Merchant Human Resource account user may only manage staff within the same Branch as the Merchant Human Resource account user. The Merchant Human Resource user must not manage staff in other Branches, assign staff to other Branches, manage Merchant Administrator account activation status, independently activate staff accounts where Merchant Administrator activation is required, create separate Merchant Administrator approval workflows as an HR authority model, export Client data, export payment data, self-escalate permissions, or assign themselves a higher-risk role.

5.3. A Merchant Human Resource user may create staff by adding the staff email address, selecting the specific Merchant account type, selecting the staff member’s specific role within that account type, assigning service eligibility where the staff member is Merchant Personnel, triggering the invite or activation email, and showing pending activation until the account is activated through the permitted lifecycle flow.

5.4. Staff account types that may be selected by Merchant Human Resource users, subject to Branch scope, Customer configuration, role permissions, and Applicable Law, may include Merchant Personnel, Merchant Front Office, Merchant Finance, and Merchant Audit.

5.5. Staff roles selected by Merchant Human Resource users may include service roles or business roles relevant to the Customer’s operations, including, where configured, Barber, Massage Therapist, Hairdresser, Stylist, Beautician, Cosmetologist, Nail Technician, Facial Therapist, Grooming Specialist, Finance Officer, Front Office User, Audit User, or other Customer-defined or system-defined roles.

5.6. Merchant Human Resource users may Process mandatory staff profile fields, including first name, last name, display name, profile picture where configured by merchant policy, email, phone, role, employment type, employment status, primary Branch, start date, and staff-invited-by records.

5.7. Servana may prevent duplicate active staff accounts within the platform by email and phone. Merchant Human Resource users may therefore trigger, view, or respond to duplicate-staff prevention records, conflict warnings, blocked creation events, or controlled staff-resolution workflows.

5.8. Merchant Human Resource users may manage personnel service eligibility for Merchant Personnel. Personnel service eligibility determines the services that Merchant Personnel may lawfully or operationally be assigned to perform within Servana and must not be replaced by manual Front Office judgment where Servana requires system-based eligibility.

5.9. Merchant Human Resource users may manage staff availability, including availability calendars, shifts, working days, working hours, breaks, off-duty status, unavailable dates, emergency unavailable status, temporary unavailability, sick leave, emergency leave, no-show status, active or inactive for queue, and related workforce availability records.

5.10. Merchant Human Resource users may suspend or deactivate staff users where permitted. Suspension or deactivation may immediately invalidate existing active sessions, invalidate unused Magic Links, block login, preserve historical operational records, preserve audit logs, and trigger reassignment checks for live queues, appointments, and service sessions.

5.11. Merchant Human Resource users may search and filter staff by staff name, email, phone, Branch, role, status, service eligibility, availability, employment status, and related staff attributes. Such search capability exists for lawful HR administration only and must not be used for unauthorised surveillance, harassment, discrimination, extraction, or disclosure.

5.12. Merchant Human Resource users may export staff roster data only. Staff roster exports must not include Client data, payment data, receipt data, invoice data, sensitive Client contact data, financial reports, or other non-HR records unless expressly permitted by another role and lawful configuration.

5.13. Merchant Human Resource users may view HR-visible audit events limited to staff and access changes. Full audit functionality remains available to Merchant Audit where permitted. Merchant Human Resource users must not treat HR-visible audit access as authority to view unrestricted operational, financial, Client, payment, receipt, invoice, or platform audit records.

5.14. Because the Merchant Human Resource role controls staff identity, staff access, availability, role assignment, and service eligibility, the Merchant Human Resource user’s actions may be extensively logged, including staff invitation, invitation resend, invitation revocation, staff profile creation, staff profile editing, employment data changes, role assignment, service eligibility assignment, availability changes, working-hour changes, branch-scoped assignment actions, suspension, deactivation, self-escalation attempts, staff roster exports, unauthorised access attempts, IP address, user agent, timestamp, target records, Branch identifier, merchant identifier, severity, and related audit metadata.
