## 4. DESCRIPTION OF SERVANA

4.1. Servana is a multi-tenant SaaS web platform for service-based SMEs, including barbershops, salons, massage parlours, spas, grooming studios, beauty parlours, and similar appointment-based or walk-in businesses.

4.2. Servana is designed to operate as a service-operations platform through which merchant businesses can digitise, control, and audit day-to-day operations, including merchant onboarding, merchant branches, staff and personnel access, front-office operations, client records, walk-ins, appointments, queue management, service delivery tracking, client choice of next available personnel or preferred personnel, invoice generation, offline payment recording, receipt generation, personnel commission visibility, client data protection, contact access control, platform fee calculation, service fee tier invoice pricing, merchant financial visibility, and audit-ready operational and financial records. 

4.3. Servana must not be treated solely as a booking application, point-of-sale system, payment processor, invoicing tool, staff roster, or reporting tool. It is a service-operations SaaS platform intended to help Customers run clients, staff, services, queues, invoices, commissions, platform fees, reports, and audit records from one secure web dashboard.

4.4. Servana is structured around tenant isolation. Each merchant operates as an isolated tenant. A merchant’s data must not be accessible, inferable, editable, exportable, or enumerable by another merchant, subject only to controlled Citrus Labs Limited platform-level workflows, lawful support access, security operations, legal compliance, billing enforcement, abuse controls, and other lawful exceptions.

4.5. Servana records offline or off-platform payments. Servana may record payment method, amount, reference, status, validation details, payment notes, partial payment records, split payment records, external refund records, cash-up records, reconciliation records, disputes, receipts, and related audit trails, but Servana does not, by this Policy alone, represent that it directly processes client-to-merchant payments inside the platform.

4.6. Servana includes front-office functions that permit the Merchant Front Office user, within the assigned Branch scope and permissions, to register Clients, retrieve existing Clients, prevent duplicate Client registration within the same Branch, create walk-in sessions, create appointment records, convert appointment arrivals into active queue and service flows without duplicating appointment, queue, or session records, select services, assign next available personnel, allow Clients to select preferred personnel at an extra cost where enabled, manage queue status, create service sessions, generate invoices, record offline payment methods, submit payment details for Finance validation, view awaiting-Finance-validation status, view ready-for-receipt status, view daily Branch activity, view current appointments and walk-ins, notify Clients of queue or service status, and search speed-sensitive operational records.

## 5. MERCHANT FRONT OFFICE ROLE AND PRIVACY TREATMENT

5.1. The Merchant Front Office account is a Customer-side client-facing operational account within Servana. Its purpose is to support Branch-level intake, appointment handling, walk-in handling, queue handling, service selection, personnel assignment, invoice initiation, offline payment recording, Finance-validation submission, Client notification, and immediate operational search.

5.2. The Merchant Front Office user is branch-scoped. Merchant Front Office users must not access other Branches externally or within the same Merchant Administrator except for the Merchant Branch into which the Merchant Front Office user has been assigned, unless Servana, the Customer, and Applicable Law expressly permit a broader scope.

5.3. The Merchant Front Office user may register Clients, retrieve existing Clients, and support same-Branch duplicate-client prevention. Client records may include name, phone number, optional email, optional gender, visit history, service history, assigned personnel history, payment history, receipt history, preferences, preferred personnel history, notes, consent records, and communication preferences.

5.4. The Merchant Front Office user may create walk-ins and appointments, including workflows that atomically create or attach the Client, selected service, Branch, queue entry, assignment mode, and optional preferred-personnel fee.

5.5. The Merchant Front Office user may convert an appointment arrival into an active queue and service flow without duplicating appointment, queue, or session records. Such workflows may involve check-in records, appointment references, Client identity, selected services, Branch, eligible personnel, queue position, service session status, invoice status, and payment status.

5.6. The Merchant Front Office user may manage actionable queue stages, including check-in, assignment, service start, invoice generation, offline payment recording, awaiting Finance validation, and ready-for-receipt status, subject to Servana’s valid state transitions and role permissions.

5.7. The Merchant Front Office user may select services and assign next available personnel, provided personnel assignment respects HR-controlled service eligibility, Branch assignment, personnel availability, service duration, queue length, and any applicable queue configuration.

5.8. The Merchant Front Office user may allow a Client to select preferred personnel at an extra cost where enabled. If preferred personnel is selected, the system may display the extra fee and estimated wait time, require Client confirmation, attach the queue entry to selected personnel, include the preferred-personnel waiting fee on the invoice, record offline payment, submit payment to Finance for validation, generate or permit receipt after validation according to applicable rules, and create audit logs recording selected personnel, fee, and the user who created the session.

5.9. The Merchant Front Office user may generate invoices and record offline payment information, but must not issue receipts without a linked invoice and valid payment state. The Merchant Front Office user may view awaiting-Finance-validation status and ready-for-receipt status, but Finance validation and receipt generation may be controlled by Merchant Finance, Merchant Administrator, or configured role-based issuance rules.

5.10. The Merchant Front Office user may view daily Branch activity, including walk-ins, appointments, completed services, pending services, invoices created, unpaid invoices, payments pending validation, and receipts issued.

5.11. The Merchant Front Office user may notify Clients of queue or service status where such functionality is enabled and lawful. Such notifications may involve Client contact details, appointment references, queue status, service status, estimated wait information, cancellation or no-show status, receipt availability, or other service communications.

5.12. The Merchant Front Office user may use speed-sensitive search, including searches by phone number, name, appointment reference, invoice number, receipt number, and queue position, subject to role, Branch, tenant, audit, masking, and lawful-use controls.

5.13. Because the Merchant Front Office role is client-facing and operational, the Merchant Front Office user’s actions may be extensively logged, including Client create and edit actions, client retrieval, duplicate-prevention events, check-in events, appointment changes, queue changes, assignment actions, preferred-personnel choices, invoice creation, offline payment recording, receipt-status views, notification actions, searches, IP address, user agent, timestamp, target records, Branch identifier, merchant identifier, severity, and related audit metadata.
