## 4. DESCRIPTION OF SERVANA

4.1. Servana is a multi-tenant SaaS web platform for service-based SMEs, including barbershops, salons, massage parlours, spas, grooming studios, beauty parlours, and similar appointment-based or walk-in businesses.

4.2. Servana is designed to operate as a service-operations platform through which merchant businesses can digitise, control, and audit day-to-day operations, including merchant onboarding, merchant branches, staff and personnel access, front-office operations, client records, walk-ins, appointments, queue management, service delivery tracking, client choice of next available personnel or preferred personnel, invoice generation, offline payment recording, receipt generation, personnel commission visibility, client data protection, contact access control, platform fee calculation, service fee tier invoice pricing, merchant financial visibility, and audit-ready operational and financial records.

4.3. Servana must not be treated solely as a booking application, point-of-sale system, payment processor, invoicing tool, staff roster, or reporting tool. It is a service-operations SaaS platform intended to help Customers run clients, staff, services, queues, invoices, commissions, platform fees, reports, and audit records from one secure web dashboard.

4.4. Servana is structured around tenant isolation. Each merchant operates as an isolated tenant. A merchant’s data must not be accessible, inferable, editable, exportable, or enumerable by another merchant, subject only to controlled Citrus Labs Limited platform-level workflows, lawful support access, security operations, legal compliance, billing enforcement, abuse controls, and other lawful exceptions.

4.5. Servana records offline or off-platform payments. Servana may record payment method, amount, reference, status, validation details, payment notes, partial payment records, split payment records, external refund records, cash-up records, reconciliation records, disputes, receipts, and related audit trails, but Servana does not, by this Policy alone, represent that it directly processes client-to-merchant payments inside the platform.

## 5. MERCHANT ADMINISTRATOR ROLE AND PRIVACY TREATMENT

5.1. The Merchant Administrator is the primary Customer-side account owner and merchant tenant administrator addressed by this Policy.

5.2. The Merchant Administrator self-registers the merchant business account. After self-registration, Servana creates the merchant tenant and assigns the registering user as Merchant Owner / Merchant Administrator. Super Administrator manual approval of Merchant Administrator self-registration is not required for dashboard access, and Servana does not require merchant KYC submission, compliance document submission, or activation document submission as part of the Merchant Administrator self-registration workflow unless a separate lawful feature, agreement, or regulatory requirement is later enabled.

5.3. After account creation, the Merchant Administrator must complete first-time setup before accessing the Merchant Administrator dashboard. First-time setup may include selecting the merchant service fee tier, completing and managing the merchant profile, creating at least one Merchant Branch account, completing the Merchant Branch profile, adding initial Merchant Branch account user email addresses, adding initial Merchant Human Resource account user email addresses, selecting the relevant Merchant Branch of operation for each initial staff email address, triggering Magic Link welcome emails for invited users, and being redirected to the Merchant Administrator dashboard after setup completion.

5.4. The Merchant Administrator may view all Merchant Branch accounts created under that Merchant Administrator, view merchant staff under those branches, view merchant-level revenue reports in real time across branches, view branch revenue performance for today, this week, last month, and the last three months, view branch services and pricing, view service revenue performance, view staff performance cumulatively per branch and per individual staff, view merchant-level platform fee records, receive daily branch day-close reports by email in PDF format, receive daily branch cash-up and reconciliation reports by email in PDF format, lock financial periods where required and permitted, and suspend inactive or abusive merchant users while preserving historical records and audit logs.

5.5. The Merchant Administrator may add only Merchant Branch account user email addresses and Merchant Human Resource account user email addresses during the Merchant Administrator-controlled staff setup flow. The Merchant Administrator must not create or approve other account users outside the permitted flow, link Merchant Personnel to accounts, configure services or service pricing at Merchant Administrator level, configure branch personnel assignments, configure Merchant Personnel commissions directly, control whether Front Office users can record payments or issue receipts, or assign personnel to branches. Those actions are controlled by other Servana roles, permissions, or workflows.

5.6. The Merchant Administrator may be treated as a representative of the Customer, an account owner, a Customer administrator, and, for Customer-controlled Personal Data, a person acting under the authority of the Customer. The Customer may be the Data Controller for many categories of Personal Data Processed in Servana, including client data, staff data, service records, appointments, invoices, offline payment records, receipts, commission records, and branch records, except where Citrus Labs Limited independently determines the relevant Processing purposes and means.
